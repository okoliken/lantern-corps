# Lantern Corps: Story Bible

A fan game. We follow the **comics and the animated series directions**: Red Lanterns are the main
enemy, and Manhunters are the second threat. The plot, missions and original characters below are
our own. Canon characters (Hal, John, the Guardians, Atrocitus, Dex-Starr, the Manhunters) are used
the way the comics and show portray them.

This file drives enemy design, missions, dialogue and the codex. If the story changes, change it here
first.

---

## Tone
Comic-book heroic. Big, bright, earnest moments ("In brightest day…"), real stakes, and humor that
comes from Hal and John's different personalities, never from undercutting the danger.

## Premise: "Red Frontier"
Green Lantern outposts along the frontier of **Sector 2814** are going dark, one by one. The Guardians
of Oa send **Hal Jordan** and **John Stewart** to find out why.

The answer is the **Red Lanterns**, an army powered by rage and led by **Atrocitus**. Long ago the
Guardians' first peacekeepers, the robotic **Manhunters**, massacred his home sector. Atrocitus has
never forgiven the Guardians, and now he's burning a path to Oa.

Worse, he's found a way to turn the Guardians' own past against them: a buried **Manhunter vault**.

---

## The emotional spectrum (the rule behind every enemy)
Each ring is powered by an emotion, and **every faction fights the way its emotion feels**. That's how
story and gameplay stay connected.

| Light | Emotion | Who | How it plays |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green | Willpower | Hal, John | Constructs: build what you imagine; focus and control |
| Red | Rage | Red Lanterns | Relentless aggression; burning plasma that eats through constructs; stronger when hurt |
| (none) | Cold logic | Manhunters | Organized, tactical, adaptive; learn and counter what you do |

## Cast

### Hal Jordan: The Test Pilot
- **Personality:** fearless, cocky, improvises, jokes under pressure, acts first and plans later.
- **Fighting style:** aggressive and fast. Constructs hit hard but don't last (see traits).
- **Arc:** learns that raw courage isn't enough when the enemy *wants* you reckless. Atrocitus
  baits him. By the end Hal chooses to hold the line instead of charging, trusting John.
- **Signature (C3):** *Jet Strike*, a construct fighter jet that dashes through enemies.

### John Stewart: The Architect
- **Personality:** former Marine and architect; disciplined, precise, protective, dry humor.
- **Fighting style:** control and defense. Sturdy structures, cheaper walls and cages.
- **Arc:** his instinct is to protect everyone. The Manhunter vault forces him to make hard calls
  about who he can save. He learns to trust Hal's improvisation when plans break.
- **Signature (C3):** *Fortress*, a construct dome with auto-turrets that shelters allies.

### Hal & John together
Banter comes from contrast: Hal wants to go in, John wants a plan; Hal teases John's "blueprints",
John reminds Hal who keeps pulling him out of trouble. In co-op, their lines react to each other.

### The Guardians of Oa
Ancient, calm, secretive. They built the Manhunters and don't like to talk about it. Mission briefings
come from them. Part of the story is getting them to admit the truth.

## Factions & bestiary
Each enemy has one clear job, so it teaches the player something. Numbers are starting points for
balancing.

### Red Lanterns (rage)
Fast and in your face. **They push you to use shields, walls and cages.**
Look: dark red and black, jagged, glowing red eyes and mouths, rings that drip plasma.
Shared trait: **Rage** builds as they take damage, making them faster and angrier.

| Enemy | Role | Behavior | Weakness | Teaches |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rage Grunt** | Basic melee | Rushes the nearest Lantern and claws. Gets **faster as it's hurt**. Comes in packs | Knockback and cages stop the rush | Movement, ring shot, spacing |
| **Plasma Spitter** | Ranged area denial | Keeps its distance and **spits burning plasma** that leaves fire puddles for a few seconds. Plasma **melts constructs faster** | Low health; chain pulls it out of hiding | Dodging, reading the ground, not over-relying on walls |
| **Rage Brute** | Heavy | Slow, huge. Winds up a **charge that smashes through energy walls** and knocks you flat | Sidestep the charge; it's stunned after hitting a wall or rock | Timing, using terrain |
| **Dex-Starr** (mini-boss) | Fast predator | The Red Lantern cat. Darts, **pounces**, retreats, repeats. Summons small rage bursts | Cages; it hates being trapped | Traps and target lock |
| **Atrocitus** (final boss) | Commander | Phase 1: rage waves and plasma storms. Phase 2: summons grunts and brutes. Phase 3: blood-red **napalm floods** the arena and he goes berserk | Shields for the floods; stay mobile | Everything |

**Original lieutenant: Skarr Vell, the Scalded** (Act 1 boss). Atrocitus's frontier captain; a burned
ex-soldier who leads raids personally. Charges like a brute, spits like a spitter. A mix of what the
act taught.

### Manhunters (cold logic)
Organized, precise, relentless. **They push you to switch constructs.**
Look: red and silver androids, a single glowing eye, flat voices ("You are in violation.").
Shared trait: **Adapt**. They share data, so the more you repeat a construct in a fight, the more
resistant nearby Manhunters get to it.

| Enemy | Role | Behavior | Weakness | Teaches |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Manhunter Drone** | Basic soldier | Patrols, alerts others, **baton swipe** up close, **eye blast** at range | Mixed constructs; big hits interrupt its attacks | Reading wind-ups, construct variety |
| **Manhunter Sentry** | Support turret | Stationary. **Shields nearby drones** and fires steady beams | Destroy it first; chain can yank drones out of its shield | Target priority, lock-on |
| **Manhunter Adapter** | Elite | **Gains heavy resistance to the construct you use most**, shown on its armor color | Keep switching constructs | Using the whole loadout |
| **Manhunter Prime** (boss) | Commander | Commands drones and sentries. Each phase it **locks out one of your constructs** it has "learned" | Improvise with what's left; shield and lock-on | Adapting under pressure |

### Three-way fights (Act 2+)
Red Lanterns and Manhunters **hate each other** and attack each other on sight. The player can hang
back and let them fight, lure one faction into the other, or get caught in the middle.

## Acts & missions
Mission types come from what's happening in the story. Planets are original; Oa and Ysmault are canon.

### Act 1: Rage at the Border
*The frontier is on fire. Find out who's attacking and why.*
1. **Silent Outpost** (planet, Kel-Aris Station): investigate a dark outpost. Tutorial: movement,
   ring shot, flying. First Rage Grunts.
2. **Colony Under Fire** (planet, Mirrow Colony): **rescue** colonists (shield them!) while Grunts
   attack. Plasma Spitters appear.
3. **The Ambush** (space, Durvan Belt): defend a damaged Green Lantern ship in the asteroid field.
   First Rage Brute.
4. **Boss: Skarr Vell** (planet, the burning outpost). His defeat reveals the Reds are hunting for
   "the Guardians' buried shame".

### Act 2: The Old Machines
*Atrocitus is after a Manhunter vault. The Guardians have been hiding something.*
5. **Briefing on Oa**: story scene. The Guardians admit they built the Manhunters.
6. **Race to the Vault** (planet, Tharsis Deep): get there before the Reds. First Manhunter Drones
   wake up. First three-way fight.
7. **The Vault Awakens** (planet, inside the vault): **escort** John as he tries to shut it down.
   Sentries and Adapters.
8. **Protect the Survivors** (space, around a colony ship): Manhunters target everyone. **Defend**
   with walls and shields.
9. **Boss: Manhunter Prime** (vault core). It locks out constructs as it learns you.

### Act 3: Blood Oath
*End it at the source.*
10. **Into Red Space** (space, the border of Sector 666): fight through Red patrols.
11. **Dex-Starr** (planet, ruined world): mini-boss hunt.
12. **Ysmault** (planet, the Red Lantern stronghold): everything Red at full strength.
13. **Final boss: Atrocitus**. After the fight, a choice of words: Hal and John don't just beat him,
    they show the Guardians' past was wrong without excusing what he did.

## Story system (how it works in the game)
Built during **C5** and the mission milestones. Everything is **data**, like the constructs.

- **Story data** (`src/lib/story/`): acts → missions → beats. Each mission names its map, objective,
  enemies, briefing scene and ending scene.
- **Scenes:** portrait and text dialogue between missions and at key moments. Lines can depend on
  who's playing (Hal, John, or both in co-op).
- **Combat lines:** short callouts triggered by events (first enemy of a type, shield cast, low
  willpower, partner downed, boss phase change). Each Lantern has their own voice.
- **Codex / bestiary:** an entry unlocks the first time you meet an enemy: lore, abilities,
  weaknesses. Also used as the in-game reference for what each enemy does.
- **Progress:** which missions are done, per save; feeds the sector map (Phase 4) and progression (C4).

## Build order for enemies
1. **Rage Grunt** (Act 1's first enemy; simplest: rush + claw + rage speed-up)
2. **Plasma Spitter** (adds ranged attacks and fire puddles)
3. **Rage Brute** (adds charge and wall smashing)
4. **Skarr Vell** (first boss, reuses the three above)
5. Manhunters: **Drone → Sentry → Adapter → Prime**
6. **Dex-Starr**, then **Atrocitus**
