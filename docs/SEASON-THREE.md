# Season Three: Parallax

Season two ended with Sinestro beaten and the great yellow lantern still
burning. The Guardians took it home to be "studied". Season three is what was
inside it, and what the Guardians always knew: Parallax, the living fear they
locked inside their own Central Battery long ago. That's why the rings were
always weak against yellow.

Drawn from the 2011 film: Parallax, Hector Hammond, Carol Ferris and the
Ferris Aircraft gala, the Guardians' fear, and the ending in the sun.

## The cast across the season

- **Hal** carries it: Oa, Coast City, and finally the sun.
- **John** holds Earth: Hammond, then Parallax's approach.
- **Kilowog** flies with Hal on Oa.
- **Sinestro** in a Sciencell knows the truth and isn't listened to. Act 2: Hal
  lets him out, because nobody knows fear better. Act 3: he fights beside Hal
  with a green ring. The very end: he puts on the yellow ring.
- **Hector Hammond**: a scientist infected by a fragment of the yellow lantern.
  Telepathy and telekinesis, growing as Parallax feeds.
- **Carol Ferris**, the Ferris Aircraft gala, Coast City.

## Acts

### Act 1: The Impurity
1. **Studied** (Hal + Kilowog, Oa). The Guardians' study of the yellow lantern
   cracks it. Fear pours out of the cracks; four containment pylons hold the
   rest in. Fear creatures go for the pylons; Hal can pour his will into a
   damaged pylon (stand by it) to mend it. At the end the lantern shatters and a
   fragment streaks away, toward Earth.
2. **The Gala** (Hal, Coast City). The Ferris Aircraft gala. Hector Hammond,
   touched by the fragment, can move things with his mind: he drops a
   helicopter on the crowd (catch it and set it down), throws the hangar's
   aircraft, and takes hold of Hal's constructs and turns them on him (use a
   different one). He floats Carol out over the drop: hit him to break his
   concentration, and catch her.
3. *(planned)* John arrives on Earth; Sinestro's warning on Oa.

### Act 2: Parallax
Parallax breaks loose and tears through the Guardians. Hal frees Sinestro.
Hammond, feeding Parallax, takes Coast City; John holds it.

### Act 3: Into the Sun
Both Lanterns, Kilowog and Sinestro against Parallax. John keeps it off
Earth; Hal drags it into the sun. Then Sinestro and the yellow ring.
