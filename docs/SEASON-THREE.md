# Season Three: Parallax

Season two ended with Sinestro beaten and the great yellow lantern still
burning. The Guardians took it home to be "studied". Season three is what was
inside it, and what the Guardians always knew: Parallax, the living fear they
locked inside their own Central Battery long ago. That's why the rings were
always weak against yellow.

Drawn from the 2011 film: Parallax, Hector Hammond, Carol Ferris and the
Ferris Aircraft gala, the Guardians' fear, and the ending in the sun.

## The cast across the season

- **Hal** carries it: Oa, Coast City, and finally the sun.
- **John** holds Earth: Hammond, then Parallax's approach.
- **Kilowog** flies with Hal on Oa.
- **Sinestro** in a Sciencell knows the truth and isn't listened to. Act 2: Hal
  lets him out, because nobody knows fear better. Act 3: he fights beside Hal
  with a green ring. The very end: he puts on the yellow ring.
- **Hector Hammond**: a scientist infected by a fragment of the yellow lantern.
  Telepathy and telekinesis, growing as Parallax feeds.
- **Carol Ferris**, the Ferris Aircraft gala, Coast City.

## Hal's new constructs

Hal can't beat Parallax with the kit he has. Every act of the season gives him
something new and original (not copied from the film), each one also going
into his Armory:

- **Carrier Strike** (The Gala): an aircraft carrier rises behind him and
  launches six jets at the crosshair; each hunts the nearest enemy there and
  bombs it.
- **Sonic Boom** (Unbound): a supersonic run along the aim, straight through
  everything; the shock cone he leaves behind hits the whole line again.
- *(planned)* More in Act 2 and Act 3 (Ace Frame, a construct mech, is saved for
  the finale), building to the fight in the sun.

## Acts

### Act 1: The Impurity
1. **Studied** (Hal + Kilowog, Oa). The Guardians' study of the yellow lantern
   cracks it. Fear pours out of the cracks; four containment pylons hold the
   rest in. Fear creatures go for the pylons; Hal can pour his will into a
   damaged pylon (stand by it) to mend it. At the end the lantern shatters and a
   fragment streaks away, toward Earth.
2. **The Gala** (Hal, Coast City). The Ferris Aircraft gala. Hector Hammond,
   touched by the fragment, can move things with his mind: he turns the show
   helicopter over the crowd (Hal flies under it and holds it up all the way
   down), flings the jets parked on the apron, and takes the
   construct in Hal's hand and turns it on him (use a different one). Half
   beaten, he lifts Carol into the sky: hit him to break his concentration,
   then catch her. Beaten, he's pulled up into the dark by something calling
   him.
3. **The Warning** (John, Coast City). Scene: on Oa, Sinestro tells Hal what
   the Guardians won't say while yellow cracks crawl up the Central Battery;
   then Coast City, people frozen in the street, the Hunger feeding. Mission:
   free the frozen people (stand by them, they run for the subway); every one
   still frozen is armour on the Hunger; it hunts and feeds on them, roars to
   freeze the runners again, and sends Fear Hounds after them.

### Act 2: Parallax
4. **Unbound** (Hal + Kilowog, Oa). Scene: the Battery bursts, Parallax rises,
   the Guardians fire on it and it takes one. Mission: Parallax looms over the
   plaza and reaches down for the six Guardians; break each tendril's grip
   before it drains them grey, dodge its slams, survive the fear things. It
   can't be beaten yet: it leaves, toward Earth. New construct: Sonic Boom.
5. *(planned)* Hal frees Sinestro. Hammond, feeding Parallax, takes Coast
   City; John holds it.

### Act 3: Into the Sun
Both Lanterns, Kilowog and Sinestro against Parallax. John keeps it off
Earth; Hal drags it into the sun. Then Sinestro and the yellow ring.
