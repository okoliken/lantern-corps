# Season Two

Season one was the Corps against rage: the frontier, the Manhunters, Oa, Ysmault
and Atrocitus. Season two goes back and forth between two lives:

- **John on Earth.** Each of his episodes is John and one member of the Justice
  League on a job, and every job teaches him something new about the ring: how
  to hold things up instead of hitting them, how to keep a construct together
  when he is scared, how to win without hurting anyone.
- **Hal in space.** Hal, usually with Kilowog, out in the sectors, and Sinestro
  slowly turning. He is the Corps' best Lantern at the start, an exile in the
  middle, and the master of a yellow ring and his own Corps at the end.

The season ends with the Sinestro Corps.

The rule underneath it: willpower is overcoming fear. Season one's enemies were
angry. Season two's are frightening, and the fights are about not letting them
get into your head.

## Act One · Two Lanterns

Two lives, and the first crack in Sinestro. No yellow yet.

1. **The Watchtower** · John, with the League. *Built.* Training on the
   Watchtower deck: the League's drills are about people, not enemies.
2. **Hard Order** · Hal and Kilowog, with Sinestro. A routine patrol: a colony
   on the edge of the sector under attack, and Sinestro in charge. He saves it,
   then keeps it in line by fear: orders, curfews, nobody steps out. Hal is
   uneasy; Kilowog says that is just Sinestro.
3. **Ground Level** · John, with Superman. A cargo lifter loses power over a full
   dock. No villain: a weight to hold up and a crowd that will not move fast
   enough. Superman takes the far end, and it is still too much for two.
4. **Korugar** · Hal, Kilowog. Sinestro's home world, kept "safe" by terror:
   his own police, his face on every wall. The Guardians order him brought in,
   and Hal has to do it. Sinestro does not come quietly. He is stripped of the
   ring and exiled.

## Act Two · Fear Spreads

Fear on Earth, a yellow ring in space, and the first time fear beats will.

5. **Gotham at Night** · John, with Batman. Scarecrow's fear gas: the first time
   John's constructs flicker and shrink because he is afraid. The dread meter
   arrives here.
6. **Qward** · Hal and Kilowog. They follow the exile into the anti-matter
   universe and find the Weaponers forging a yellow ring for him.
7. **What Grodd Wants** · John, with the Flash and Hawkgirl. Grodd wants the
   ring, and he gets inside John's head.
8. **First Light of Fear** · Hal. Sinestro comes back wearing yellow. One on
   one, and Hal loses. The act ends there.

## Act Three · The Sinestro Corps

Yellow rings go looking for the fearful.

9. **Recruited** · John, with Wonder Woman. A yellow ring finds a frightened man
   on Earth. He is not a villain and you cannot kill him.
10. **The Rings Go Out** · Hal and Kilowog. Yellow rings spread across the
    sectors, turning people. Get the Lanterns out before they are picked off.
11. **Two Lanterns** · John and Hal, together at last. The Sinestro Corps hits
    the Watchtower.
12. **Sinestro** · the finale: Sinestro and his lieutenants. It ends with the
    yellow lantern still burning, a seed for season three.

## Yellow Lanterns: fear, not rage

Red Lanterns are brute rage: they close in, hit hard and spit blood plasma.
Yellow Lanterns are frightening and clever.

- **Dread.** Yellow attacks (and Scarecrow's gas) put dread on you. The more
  dread, the smaller and weaker your constructs come out and the darker the
  edges of the screen. You push it back by landing hits and holding your
  shield: willpower beating fear. It never stops you playing.
- **Nightmare constructs.** Talons, spiked chains, insect swarms, spiked cages,
  and fear shapes that look like enemies you have already beaten.
- **Sinestro.** Fear cage, yellow lance, and mirror constructs: your own shapes
  thrown back at you in yellow.
- **Fair, always.** Shields hold, nothing one-shots you, and every big attack
  has a tell. Same rule as season one.

## Who is built

- Allies ready to go: Superman, Wonder Woman, the Flash, Hawkgirl. Hal, John,
  Kilowog and Sinestro (green) are drawn.
- To build: Batman as an ally (he exists only as a sparring opponent), Scarecrow,
  the Yellow Lanterns (Lantern figures in yellow and black, plus yellow
  constructs), Sinestro in yellow, and the Korugar, Qward and Gotham maps.
- Green Arrow is not in this game. He can come later as a bonus episode.

## Build order

1. Act One: Hard Order, Ground Level, Korugar. Nothing yellow needed.
2. The dread system and Scarecrow, then Batman as an ally: Gotham at Night.
3. Yellow Lanterns and Sinestro in yellow: Qward, First Light of Fear.
4. The rest of Act Two, then Act Three.

Each act is played and tuned before the next one starts, like season one.
