# Season Two — "The Home Sector"

Season one was the Corps: the frontier, Oa, Ysmault, Atrocitus. Season two is
not a Green Lantern story. It is a **John Stewart story**, on Earth, with the
Justice League, and the ring is a tool he brings to their work rather than the
thing the story is about. No Guardians. No sector. No Corps politics.

You play **John** throughout.

## The shape of the season

- **Act 1 — Join.** John comes home, trains with the League, and learns that
  their work is not the Corps' work: civilians in every fight, no orders, and a
  team that argues.
- **Act 2 — Serve.** Earth's own problems, escalating. He is one of them now.
- **Act 3 — The turn.** The League business finishes. Then, in the last mission,
  a yellow ring reaches Earth, and word comes that Sinestro has stopped
  answering Oa. That is the whole seed, and it lands in the closing minutes.
- **Season three** is what that seed grows into: Sinestro's corps, the Lanterns
  under attack, and John going back up.

The rule the season argues, under every mission: on Oa the job is to win. On
Earth the job is to make sure nobody dies while you win, and John is not used
to that.

## What he gains

Season one's constructs were a soldier's: walls, hammers, cages. Earth work
needs different shapes, so **new constructs unlock through the act**, each from
a mission that made him need it.

| construct | from | what it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cradle | Watchtower | a net that catches falling people without hurting them |
| Brace | Ground Level | a held support that drains while it holds something up |
| Lattice | What Grodd Wants | a wide shield that covers a street, not a man |
| Cuff | Checkmate | binds a person without harming them, for enemies you cannot kill |
| Mirror | Amazo | throws a copied power back at whatever sent it |

## Act One, mission by mission

**1. The Watchtower** · training with the League
The first mission is a training floor, not a fight: the League runs him through
their drills, and their drills are about people, not enemies. Flash sets the
pace, Wonder Woman does not go easy on him. Ends with him being offered the
comm, which he does not take yet.
*New work:* a drill director built on the existing sparring code, the Cradle,
and the Watchtower room. No new enemies at all.

**2. Ground Level** · Coast City, the harbour, with Superman
A cargo lifter loses power over a full dock. No enemy, no fight. Hold it up with
the Brace while the cranes clear beneath it, and the crowd will not move fast
enough. Superman takes the far end, and the weight is still too much for two.
*New work:* the Brace, a willpower drain that bites, civilians who scatter badly.

**3. What Grodd Wants** · Central City, with the Flash and Hawkgirl
Gorilla City tech is loose in the streets and the Flash is already chasing it.
Grodd is here for John's ring, and he gets into his head: while he holds you,
your constructs come out as the wrong shape. The Lattice is what saves the
street when the shooting starts.
*New work:* the mind-hold as a player state, using Manhunter Prime's construct
lockouts. All the enemy art exists from Act 2.

**4. Checkmate** · a hangar in Washington, with Wonder Woman
A human task force decided a year ago that the man with the ring is a foreign
power. They are not villains: they are frightened people with a budget and
weapons built to break constructs. You cannot kill them, and Diana will not let
you leave. The mission ends in a conversation.
*New work:* enemies whose weapons weaken green, a no-kill fail state, the Cuff.

**5. Amazo** · the old launch site, with all four
The League's own powers turned against them, one at a time. It copies whoever
last hit it, so the fight is about choosing what to teach it, and the Mirror is
how you send it back. Everyone turns up.
*New work:* a boss director on Manhunter Prime's learning code, and the Mirror.
The hardest tuning in the act and the best payoff.

## Pairings

Every mission has one or two of them beside him, and who it is changes how it
plays: Superman for weight, the Flash for ground he cannot cover, Hawkgirl for
anything that flies, Wonder Woman for anything he is not allowed to punch.

## Open

**Batman.** He is the right face for Checkmate and for most of Act 2, and he is
not built. One figure on the skeleton we already have, black and grey, no powers
to animate: about a day. My recommendation is yes, and to hold him back until
Act 2 so his arrival means something.
