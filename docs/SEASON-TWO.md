# Season Two — Act One: "The Home Sector"

Season one was the galaxy: the frontier, Oa, Ysmault, Atrocitus. Season two is
Earth. John Stewart comes home to a planet that has spent a year noticing that
its sky has a Green Lantern in it, and to a Justice League that works differently
from the Corps: no Guardians, no orders, no sector to answer to, and civilians
in every fight.

You play **John** throughout. The act's argument is simple and runs under every
mission: on Oa the job is to win. On Earth the job is to make sure nobody dies
while you win, and John is not used to that.

## What it reuses

Almost everything. The planet environment, the Justice League allies already
built (Flash, Hawkgirl, Superman, Wonder Woman), the gorilla soldiers and Grodd
art from Act 2, the Manhunter enemies, the escort and protect systems from Safe
Passage and Into Sector 666, and the boss that learns your most-used construct
from Manhunter Prime. Three of the five missions need no new enemy art at all.

## The five missions

**1. Ground Level** · Coast City, the harbour
John's first day back. A cargo lifter loses power over a full dock. No enemy, no
fight: hold the thing up with a construct while the cranes clear beneath it, and
the crowd will not move fast enough. Teaches the season's rule in three minutes.
*New work:* a held-weight construct that drains willpower while it holds, and
civilians who scatter badly. Everything else exists.

**2. What Grodd Wants** · Central City
Gorilla City tech is loose in the streets and the Flash is already chasing it.
Grodd is not here for the city, he is here for John's ring, and he gets into your
head: the psychic swirl from Act 2 returns, and while he holds you your constructs
come out as the wrong shape. Flash and Hawkgirl fight beside you.
*New work:* the mind-hold as a player state, borrowing Manhunter Prime's construct
lockouts. Enemy art all exists.

**3. Checkmate** · Washington, a hangar
A human task force decided a year ago that the man with the ring is a foreign
power. They have yellow-spectrum weapons built from Manhunter parts, and they are
not villains: they are frightened people with a budget. You cannot kill them.
Wonder Woman is with you, which makes it harder, not easier.
*New work:* enemies whose weapons weaken green constructs, a no-kill fail state,
and the first mission that ends in a conversation rather than a corpse.

**4. The Fear Engine** · Gotham, the docks at night
Something yellow came back from Sector 666 in a cargo hold. A ring without a
bearer is looking for one, and it is working through the people at the docks.
Dark, close quarters, a crowd you have to keep out of it.
*New work:* the yellow ring as an object that moves between hosts, fear as a
status effect on John himself, and a night-docks map. This is the seed of the
season, and the only mission that needs new enemy art.

**5. Amazo** · The old Watchtower launch site
The League's own powers turned against them, one at a time. It copies whoever
last hit it, so the fight is about choosing what to teach it. The whole League
turns up for the finale and you fight beside all four.
*New work:* mostly a boss director, built on Manhunter Prime's learning code.
The hardest tuning job in the act, and the best payoff.

## What I need from you before code

1. **Batman.** He is the obvious face for missions three and four and he is not
   built. He is one figure on the skeleton we already have, in black and grey,
   with no powers to animate. A day of work. In or out?
2. **Order.** Grodd is the most fun and the least new code, so it could open the
   act instead of Ground Level. I prefer Ground Level, because the act needs to
   teach the rule before it breaks it.
3. **Length.** Five missions matches Act 1 and Act 3. Six if Batman is in.
